Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026

Nova Coding Keyboard ("the App") is developed by Nova Shang. This policy describes how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • No keystrokes are recorded or logged.
  • No text you type is sent to any server.
  • No analytics, crash reports, or usage data are collected.
  • No network connections are made by the keyboard extension.

Open Access

The keyboard extension does not request Open Access (full network access). It operates entirely within the iOS sandbox with no ability to communicate with any external service.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
